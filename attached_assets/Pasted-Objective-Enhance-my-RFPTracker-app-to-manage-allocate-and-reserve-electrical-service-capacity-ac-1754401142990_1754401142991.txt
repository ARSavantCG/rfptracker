Objective:
Enhance my RFPTracker app to manage, allocate, and reserve electrical service capacity across multiple industrial properties. Each property has an FPL-provided transformer with a defined capacity (kVA), feeding multiple main panels that distribute power to tenant bays. I need a system that ensures we never overcommit electrical capacity when preparing RFPs, LOIs, or leases.

The system should enable me to:
	1.	Track the total transformer capacity (kVA) per property.
	2.	Map all main panels (with their individual capacity) connected to that transformer.
	3.	Associate tenant bays with their respective main panels.
	4.	Reserve electrical capacity (kVA) per tenant at lease signing.
	5.	Pre-reserve (“soft hold”) capacity for active RFPs and leasing proposals.
	6.	Auto-calculate remaining available capacity — updated dynamically as deals are signed or released.
	7.	Trigger alerts/warnings if a new RFP exceeds available capacity.
	8.	Maintain a reservation log with timestamps, tenant names, and kVA reserved.

Desired Features:
	•	Hierarchical structure: Property > Transformer > Main Panels > Tenant Bays.
	•	Capacity split between “Hard Allocations” (signed leases) and “Soft Holds” (pending RFPs).
	•	“Power Bank” dashboard showing:
	•	Total Transformer Capacity.
	•	Allocated Capacity.
	•	Reserved (Soft Holds) Capacity.
	•	Remaining Available Capacity.
	•	Visual indicator (like a progress bar or heatmap) showing how “full” the transformer is.
	•	Flexible enough to handle:
	•	Multiple transformers per property.
	•	Shared main panels across bays.
	•	Simple data entry form for inputting tenant kVA needs during RFP creation.

Technical Notes:
	•	The app already tracks properties, leases, and bays.
	•	This feature should integrate with the existing RFP workflow — when I draft a new RFP, I want to assign a kVA allocation.
	•	Once an RFP converts to a signed lease, the “Soft Hold” becomes a “Hard Allocation.”
	•	System should prevent overbooking and alert me if there’s not enough capacity left.

Outcome:
The system becomes my single source of truth for electrical capacity availability when issuing RFPs. It ensures I don’t promise more power than what’s actually available on the transformer and allows me to manage active deals in real time.
